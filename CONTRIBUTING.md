# Contribute

Overall guidance on contributing to a PyAnsys library appears in the
[Contributing] topic in the *PyAnsys developer's guide*. Ensure that you
are thoroughly familiar with this guide before attempting to contribute to
the PyAnsys Developer's Guide.

The following contribution information is specific to the PyAnsys Developer's Guide.

[Contributing]: https://dev.docs.pyansys.com/how-to/contributing.html

## AI-assisted contributions

The proposed [AI use policy](AI_POLICY.rst) describes human responsibility,
disclosure, validation, confidentiality, and licensing for AI-assisted
contributions. It is a draft for discussion and approval, not an official
organization-wide policy. Review it when providing feedback on the proposed
requirements.
